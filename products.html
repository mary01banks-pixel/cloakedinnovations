What non-pharmaceutical & equipment supplies does Cloaked Innovations Limited offer?

Cloaked Innovations Limited works across six categories: medical consumables, diagnostic supplies, personal protective equipment (PPE), first aid supplies, hospital and clinic supplies, and laboratory supplies. Availability within each category is confirmed directly with our team at enquiry stage.

How pricing works

We don't publish fixed price lists, because pricing depends on the specific products, quantities, and delivery requirements for your organisation. Instead, share your requirements through the enquiry form and our team will respond with a customised quotation — including for bulk and wholesale orders.
