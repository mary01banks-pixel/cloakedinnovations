What we do

Non-pharmaceutical & equipment supply

Supply across six categories: medical consumables, diagnostic and testing supplies, PPE, first aid and safety supplies, hospital, clinic and institutional supplies, and laboratory supplies.

Product sourcing enquiries

Looking for a specific item? Tell us what you need and we will check whether we can source it. Availability is confirmed at enquiry stage.

Institutional procurement support

For procurement teams with ongoing or recurring needs, we respond to enquiries and quotation requests in a clear, documented way.

Business supply solutions

Supply for corporate organisations and businesses, including safety and first aid items for workplaces.

Quotation requests

Pricing depends on the products, quantities and delivery needs, so we prepare a quotation for your requirements, including bulk and wholesale orders.

What to include in your enquiry

The more detail you share, the faster we can respond accurately.

  • The items or product category you need
  • Quantities, and whether the order is one-off or recurring
  • Your delivery location in Kenya
  • The timeline you are working to
  • Your organisation name and the best contact person

Questions about our services

What services does Cloaked Innovations Limited offer?

Cloaked Innovations Limited offers non-pharmaceutical and equipment supply, product sourcing enquiries, institutional procurement support and quotation requests for organisations and businesses in Kenya.

How do I request a quotation?

Send your requirements through the contact form, by phone on 0796 968 241, or by email, and the team will review them and respond with a quotation. Include the items, quantities, delivery location and timeline if you have them.

Do you sell to individuals?

Our focus is institutional procurement rather than one-off retail, so we mainly work with hospitals, clinics, schools, NGOs, government and private institutions, and businesses.

Can you confirm that a product is available?

Availability is confirmed at enquiry stage. Tell us the product and quantity you need and we will confirm what we can supply before you decide.
