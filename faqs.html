Answers to common questions about Cloaked Innovations Limited and how to work with us.

Cloaked Innovations Limited is a Kenyan company that sells and supplies non-pharmaceutical & equipment supplies and related products to institutions, organisations, healthcare facilities and other businesses.

We work across categories including medical consumables, diagnostic supplies, personal protective equipment, first aid supplies, hospital and clinic supplies, and laboratory supplies. Contact us to confirm current availability for your specific requirements.
