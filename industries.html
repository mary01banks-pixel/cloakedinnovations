Industries we serve

Supporting institutions & organisations across Kenya

Cloaked Innovations Limited supplies non-pharmaceutical and equipment products to hospitals, clinics, healthcare institutions, NGOs, schools, corporate businesses and procurement departments across Kenya.

Who does Cloaked Innovations supply in Kenya?

Hospitals & clinics

Hospitals and clinics can submit enquiries for hospital supplies in Kenya relevant to their day-to-day operations, and our team responds with a quotation based on confirmed requirements.

Healthcare institutions

Broader healthcare institutions can reach out with recurring or one-off supply needs, and we work to understand and respond to those requirements.

NGOs & organisations

Non-governmental organisations coordinating health-related programmes can request quotations for NGO medical supplies and the non-pharmaceutical & equipment items their work requires.

Schools & educational institutions

Schools and educational institutions can enquire about school first aid supplies and general non-pharmaceutical & equipment supplies to support their facilities.

Corporate businesses

Businesses sourcing corporate first aid and PPE supplies, or other workplace medical and health-related items, can submit their requirements through our enquiry process.

Procurement departments

Procurement teams managing structured supply processes, including bulk medical supplies, are welcome to contact us directly to discuss their organisation's requirements.

Common questions

Which industries does Cloaked Innovations Limited supply?

We supply hospitals and clinics, healthcare institutions, NGOs, schools and educational institutions, corporate businesses, and procurement departments, as well as government and private institutions across Kenya.

Can schools buy first aid supplies from Cloaked Innovations?

Yes. Schools and educational institutions can enquire about school first aid supplies and general non-pharmaceutical and equipment supplies for their facilities.

Can procurement departments request bulk medical supplies?

Yes. Procurement teams managing structured supply processes, including bulk medical supplies, are welcome to contact us directly to discuss their organisation's requirements.

How does an organisation get a quotation?

Submit your requirements through the contact form, or call 0796 968 241 or email info@cloakedinnovations.co.ke. Our team reviews your request and responds with a quotation based on confirmed requirements.
